# n-gram agreement with the reference: the app against off-the-shelf Whisper (2026-10-26)

`scripts/corpus/ngram_scores.py --summaries ~/recordings/2026-10-25_05b21a2` over the three gold call-sets
(CS001, CS008, CS014; 9 bursts, 594 reference words). For each transcriber: WER, and the precision and
recall of its 1- to 4-grams against the reference (share of the candidate's n-grams found in the reference,
and share of the reference's n-grams found in the candidate). "avg n-gram F" is the mean over n of the F
score; 4-gram recall is the share of whole phrases that came through intact, which is what the report model
actually reads.

Candidates (from `draft_reference.py`): `groq_turbo` = Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo with no prompt;
`groq_turbo_radio` = the same with the app's radio-phrase hint; `groq_large` = Groq whisper-large-v3, no
prompt; `local_medium` = faster-whisper medium on the Jetson, no prompt; `vosk_small` = the caption model
alone; `stage3` = the corpus pipeline's Groq transcript; `app (app run)` = the app's own final transcript
from the replay recordings of commit 05b21a2 (Groq turbo with the hint, then the transcript corrector and
checks).

## Reading

- **Ours is ahead of off-the-shelf on this set.** The hint alone takes Groq turbo from 20 % to 14 % WER and
  from 0.72 to 0.75 average n-gram F; the full app transcript lands at 17 % and 0.744, between the two,
  with the best 4-gram recall (0.60, tied). The corrector's changes are small on these three calls.
- **Bigger is not better.** Groq large-v3 (21 %, 0.68) is behind large-v3-turbo on radio audio, and
  local medium beats both Groq models on CS014 (17 % against 25 % and 32 %); the cloud is not a free win.
- **Vosk alone is captions, not a transcript** (69 % WER, 0.22): the safety-net role is right, the
  transcript role is not.
- **Three call-sets is a pilot number.** The generated gold set and the other 19 call-sets (once they
  have references) turn this into the standing comparison the fine-tuning plan requires: every change has
  to beat the no-prompt Groq turbo and the no-prompt local model on these columns before it ships.
