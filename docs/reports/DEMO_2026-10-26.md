Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b` as the report writer and once with the local `qwen3:4b-instruct` on the
Jetson. Transcription was Groq Whisper (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`) in both. The recordings, their
summaries and reports sit side by side under `~/demo/<CS>/` (`groq.*`, `ollama.*`), gathered by
`scripts/corpus/collect_demo.py` from `~/recordings/`; the batch was `scripts/corpus/demo_batch.py`
(started 2026-10-25 22:02 on 05b21a2, finished 2026-10-26 10:28 on d1f4373, around a server restart
and Groq's daily token cap). Every recording used here passed the audio/video sync check and had no
provider fallback.

## What the batch found in the pipeline (fixed in d1f4373)

- CS010 and CS013 carry a 75 ms 1 kHz radio beep inside the call audio (S021_01 at 13.0 s). The
  sync detector counted it as a click, so every recording of them failed with "3 clicks, 2 flashes",
  and the batch re-recorded CS010 on Groq seven times overnight against the token cap. The check
  now lists such a tone under `stray_clicks` and passes when every expected flash has its click.
- Recordings were named `<folder>/<CS>.mp4`, so the Groq retries of CS010 overwrote the finished
  local recording in the same folder. They are `<CS>.<provider>.mp4` now.
- The server had been started from a session shell and died silently at 00:12 (nothing in its log);
  the batch waited nine hours on "connection refused". It is started with `setsid nohup` now.
- Groq's 200k tokens-per-day cap closed at about 22:00 on 10-25 and was open again by 09:26 on
  10-26; the batch's interleaving (local call-sets while Groq is closed) worked as designed.
