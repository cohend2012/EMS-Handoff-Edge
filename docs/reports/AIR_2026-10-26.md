## What the air path costs

- **Word error roughly doubles** (12 to 15 %, 32 to 30 %, 17 to 45 %): consonants go, numbers mostly
  survive. On CS014 the air run is no worse than the sink run; its 30 % is the call, not the mic.
- **Level and ETA held** on all three where stated: priority 2 gave YELLOW, priority 1 gave RED, ETAs 5
  and 10 came through. CS014's ETA 5 against the gold's 10 is a judgment call on "about a five, hmm,
  ten minute ETA"; the sink runs said 10.
- **No hallucinations** in any of the three reports.
- **CS001 lost its vitals.** The transcript skipped from "sudden change in vision in his left eye" to
  "no other complaints", dropping LAMS, Cincinnati, blood sugar 94, HR 70, SpO2 97 and BP 194/110 (the 4
  omissions, score 79.6 against 91.8). Vosk's live captions do carry that sentence, so the audio reached the mic and Whisper dropped it. In its place Whisper wrote "LAMS, CINCinnati, last known
  well", the last three words being the app's radio-phrase hint: on degraded audio the hint can stand
  in for what was said. That is the same effect seen on the silent-mic captures earlier today.

## Hardware notes

- The dongle is the speaker and the mic. Mic sensitivity is low (room speech about -27 dBFS at maximum
  boost) and the 60 Hz hum sat 10 dB above it; the capture high-pass (HANDOFF_MIC_HIGHPASS_HZ, 150 Hz
  default) is what makes the mic usable.
- The dongle must hang off the main hub directly; behind the VIA hub and the Belkin adapter it was
  silent in both directions.
- The live session's 25 s room-audio test earlier produced a PSYCHIATRIC YELLOW report with an invented
  ETA 12 from a TV in the room: the round 3 ETA guard (null unless minutes are stated) has a live case.
